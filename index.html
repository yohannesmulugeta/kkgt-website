01 / WHO WE ARE

We cultivate ideas for growth.

An Ethiopian trading company built around agriculture, quality and long-term relationships.

Kelbesa Kekeba General Trading (KKGT Import Export) operates across export, import and agricultural supply. The company exports Ethiopian Arabica coffee and agricultural commodities while importing and distributing agricultural inputs and other trading products.

Our role is simple: understand market needs, source responsibly, protect quality and move products efficiently from origin to customer.
