KKGT Import Export is Kelbesa Kekeba General Trading, an Ethiopian business operating across agricultural export, crop-protection distribution and diversified import trading.

01 / COMPANY PROFILE

Trading capability anchored in agriculture.

The current KKGT company profile states that the business was established in 1999 EC. Its core activities include exporting premium Ethiopian Arabica coffee, sesame, common beans and soybeans, while importing and distributing agricultural inputs, stationery and construction materials.

Rather than presenting these as unrelated activities, KKGT operates around a common commercial discipline: understand demand, build dependable supplier relationships, protect quality and move products efficiently.
